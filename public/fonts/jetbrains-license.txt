Modification note
-----------------

`jetbrains-mono-variable.woff2` in this directory is a SUBSET of the upstream
JetBrains Mono variable font, produced with fontTools (pyftsubset). The weight
axis (wght 100-800) and coding ligatures are preserved; glyph coverage is
reduced to the Unicode ranges code blocks and data readouts on this site use
(Latin-1, common punctuation and symbols, currency, arrows, math operators,
box-drawing and block elements). The SIL OFL permits this modification. The
subset remains under the SIL OFL 1.1 and must not be sold on its own.

That subset is now split again into jetbrains-mono-latin.woff2 and
jetbrains-mono-rest.woff2 by scripts/subset-fonts.py, so a page loads only the
half its text falls into (see the unicode-range pairs in src/styles/globals.css).
Together the two files cover exactly what jetbrains-mono-variable.woff2 covers.
Both remain under the SIL OFL 1.1 and must not be sold on their own.
