01 — YouTube facade

Click a card to actually load it. Posters point straight at i.ytimg.com here; in production they are minted through the signed static proxy. maxresdefault with an hqdefault fallback on error — the second card exercises that path.
Real playback needs an http origin — the IFrame API’s origin check can’t pass from file://, so opening this lab off disk always lands in the unreachable branch (which is itself worth looking at). Serve it instead: bun dev -- --background, then /dev/embed-lab/.

The pre-play state is where the ugliness lives: the oversized red button, the title bar, the share chrome. Everything above the click is ours now.

A reader who scrolls past this is never touched by YouTube’s JS, its cookies, or its ads. That is the whole win, and it is worth being honest that it is the only one — playback after the click is still YouTube’s, ads included.
